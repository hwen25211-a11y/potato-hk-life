Genuine alpha transparency, not a white or checkerboard background. No humans, no photorealistic textures, no pixel art.

PotatoWalk / PotatoWave (v0.1.2 animation strips)
Made with agent-sprite-forge 0.4.0 through the Codex (local CLI) image_gen route, using the PotatoAtlas backpack pose as the identity reference. Each is one 2x2 sheet on a flat blue key, keyed, sliced and registered by generate2dsprite (matte QA: 0 opaque key px, 0 edge spill; sheet_qc warned safe_frame: art closer than 15% to the cell edge, not crossing), then packed into a 4-frame horizontal strip. Prompts: ArtSource/forge/prompts/walk.txt and wave.txt; raw sheets and QA in ArtSource/forge.
Files: Assets/Resources/Art/PotatoWalk.png (door walk-out, 9 fps), PotatoWave.png (home-again wave, 6 fps). Plain outfit only; costumes keep their still pose with code-driven breathing.
